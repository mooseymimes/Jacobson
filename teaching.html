Teaching

Dr. Jacobson's lectures are less instructional sessions and more akin to witnessing intellectual lightning harnessed. Enrollment is a privilege; attendance, a transformation. Expect no hand-holding, no pandering to simplistic notions.

The subject, settled. Supply, demand, and the humbling distance between what the student believes and what is true. A full syllabus is provided, as binding intellectual contracts should be in writing.
