About the Author

Few individuals can claim to not only master a field but to fundamentally redefine its very contours. Dr. Paul Jacobson is, by any objective measure (particularly his own), one such colossus of economic thought.

As a professor, his lectures are less instructional sessions and more akin to witnessing intellectual lightning harnessed. His doctorate in economics was merely the starting gun for a career that has consistently left lesser scholars scrambling to catch up to his trailblazing insights.

In his publications, as with all his works, expect no hand-holding, no pandering to simplistic notions. Instead, prepare for a rigorous, unflinching, and ultimately definitive exploration that will leave you wondering how you ever understood economics before encountering the mind of Jacobson.

“My doctorate was merely the starting gun.”
— Dr. Paul Jacobson, about Dr. Paul Jacobson
