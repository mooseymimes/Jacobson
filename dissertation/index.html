The magnum opus

The Miracle on the Han River

A rigorous, unflinching, and ultimately definitive deconstruction of the Republic of Korea's economic metamorphosis — from post-conflict agrarian substrate to global epicenter of technological innovation.

Begin at Chapter I

Abstract

In the Author's Words

This manuscript investigates the pronounced macroeconomic trajectory of the Republic of Korea (ROK), a geopolitical entity frequently designated by the heuristic “Miracle on the Han River.” The objective is to deconstruct and quantify the extensive economic metamorphosis observed over the preceding quinquagenarian period.

The initial conditions describe a system characterized by post-conflict political disequilibria and a predominantly agrarian economic substrate. This system underwent a rapid phase transition, culminating in its contemporary status as a global epicenter of technological innovation. Notwithstanding these achievements, emergent structural perturbations — notably, sub-replacement fertility rates, escalating cost-of-living indices, and a pronounced economic concentration within chaebol conglomerates — threaten to introduce non-negligible drag coefficients on future growth potentiation.

To elucidate the underlying dynamics, a multifaceted quantitative analysis is deployed: examinations of GDP temporal series, demographic state variables, growth accounting decomposition, and development accounting frameworks.
