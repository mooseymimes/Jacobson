The Journal of Jacobson Studies · Vol. 1

Scholarly Articles

The complete archive of Dr. Jacobson's shorter interventions: rebuttals, referee reports, corrections of colleagues, corrections of strangers, and the occasional unsolicited verdict. The collection grows faster than any navigation menu could bear — hence this archive, searchable and infinite in principle, like the doctor's patience is not.

Each entry is granted its own page and, where the material demands it, its own design. Scholarship this varied cannot be expected to wear a uniform.
